## What happened

During the Docker/CLIProxy recovery, `logs-max-total-size-mb` was changed from 12288 to 1024. CLIProxy hot-reloaded the setting and immediately reported `logging: removed 7773 old log file(s)` at its internal logger timestamp `2026-08-27 23:28:05`. The cap was changed to `0` one minute later, disabling the delete-based cleaner. Time Machine had no configured destination or local snapshot, and no duplicate raw CLIProxy request-log tree was found.

The deleted request logs cannot be recreated byte-for-byte. The archive therefore separates exact raw survivors from downstream application history and never labels the latter as raw HTTP recovery.

## Ongoing retention

CLIProxy's internal size cleaner stays disabled. `com.rever.cliproxy-log-archive` runs every five minutes and considers only closed logs at least one hour old. It copies to TheCloud, verifies SHA-256, records the manifest entry, rechecks the unchanged source fingerprint, and only then prunes the local copy. The active `main.log`, recent requests, Codex session JSONL, and T3's live database are not pruning targets.

The first archive-before-prune run preserved and then locally pruned 840 closed request logs with zero failures. About 21 MB remained locally: the active `main.log` and recent requests.
